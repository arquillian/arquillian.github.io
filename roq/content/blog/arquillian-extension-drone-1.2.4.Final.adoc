= Arquillian Drone Extension 1.2.4.Final Released
:sectids!:

// Historical interpolation #{site.project_space} = http://community.jboss.org/en/arquillian from https://arquillian.org/blog/2014/03/05/arquillian-extension-drone-1-2-4-Final/ (captured 2026-11-13).

image:/images/blog/drone_400.png[alt="",role="graphic pull-right"]

This is a community driven bugfix release. If you have updated to Arquillian Core 1.1.3.Final, make sure to update Drone to this version.

I’d like to thank everybody involved in this release, especially thanks to Juraj Húska and Tilmann Kuhn. _You guys make testing a breeze!_

[discrete]
=== Drone 2.0.0.Alpha1 coming soon

Selenium 2.40.0 dropped support for pass:[<code>AndroidDriver</code>] and pass:[<code>iPhoneDriver</code>]. We believe that this change should not affect Drone users that much, +
therefore we aim to make link:/modules/droidium-extension/[Arquillian Droidium] (based on http://selendroid.io/[Selendroid]), http://ios-driver.github.io/ios-driver/[ios-driver] and http://appium.io/[Appium] a drop-in replacement for Android and iOS browser testing. While we try to maintain backwards compatibility of Drone, the underlying changes to make this happen will form a baseline for the Drone 2.0.0 release. Roadmap coming soon. We’ll need your help to make the transition smooth and preserve backwards compatibility, so stay tuned!

[discrete]
==== What happens with Drone 1.x ?

We’ll drop non-supported browsers (Android, iOS) in Drone 1.3.0.Final. Because we’ll keep releasing Selenium BOM, you will be able to https://community.jboss.org/wiki/HowDoISpecifyTheSeleniumVersionInArquillianDrone[update Selenium to get latest browsers supported]. Apart from Selenium updates, the Drone 1.x branch will only get security and critical bug fixes.

We hope that you’ll enjoy our new stuff and look forward to hear your feedback in the http://community.jboss.org/en/arquillian[community forums].
