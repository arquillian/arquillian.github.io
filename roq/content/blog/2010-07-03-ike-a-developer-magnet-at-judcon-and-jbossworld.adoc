= Ike, a developer magnet at JUDCon & JBossWorld
:sectids!:

Last month, the http://jboss.org/arquillian[Arquillian] developers got together at http://www.redhat.com/summit[Red Hat Summit / JBossWorld 2010], hosted at the Seaport World Trade Center in Boston, to Experience Open Source with our community.

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/redhatsummit2010/conference-entrance.jpg" title="Conference entrance" alt="Conference entrance" />]

We http://community.jboss.org/message/532470#532470[asked the community] what they wanted to hear about at http://jboss.org/events/JUDCon.html[JUDCon]. Survey said…"`Arquillian.`" So we unleashed Ike and his fleet of testable containers onto JUDCon and JBoss World.

While Arquillian only appeared twice on the official schedule—once at JUDCon, presented by Aslak and myself, and once at JBoss World, presented by ALR—it managed to make an appearance every hour, in one form or another (including bar discussions). Thanks for encouraging him with all the votes!

On the eve of JUDCon, three of the Arquillian ringleaders (https://community.jboss.org/people/aslak[Aslak], https://community.jboss.org/people/alrubinger[ALR] and https://community.jboss.org/people/dan.j.allen[Dan]) met up at Harvard Square bar for the Boston JBUG to devise a plan to make testing a main focal point of the weeklong conference event:

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/judcon2010/arquillian-ringleader-meeting.jpg" title="Arquillian team meetup" alt="Arquillian team meetup" />]

Not letting a moment slip by, Arquillian led off the http://jboss.org/events/JUDCon/schedule.html[community track] at JUDCon early the next morning.

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/judcon2010/community-track.jpg" title="JUDCon 2010 Community Track" alt="JUDCon 2010 Community Track" />]

Aslak and I wanted to leave a strong impression on the audience. So instead of going on about theory, we prepared a talk on https://jboss.org/dms/judcon/presentations/JUDCon2010_FutureofJavaEETesting.pdf[the future of Java EE testing] that can be summed up as: http://github.com/arquillian/arquillian-showcase[demo&#44; demo&#44; demo&#44; demo]. With me driving the commands (doing my best to type on a http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keyboard_layout#Norwegian[Norwegian keyboard layout]), Aslak pointed at the screen and, in a flash, showed how Arquillian can make child’s play out of testing EJBs, JMS, JAX-RS, JPA and even JSF.

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/judcon2010/aslak-presenting-arquillian-test_scaled.jpg" title="Aslak explaining an Arquillian test" alt="Aslak explaining an Arquillian test" />]

While we’ve come a long way with Arquillian, presenting always gets us thinking of other things we can do with it. (Notice Aslak sporting the http://www.cafepress.com/jbossorg.457236901[black Arquillian shirt]).

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/judcon2010/aslak-pondering-question.jpg" title="Aslak answering a question" alt="Aslak answering a question" />]

Ike didn’t want to get stuck inside the computer the whole conference, so we printed him on a bunch of http://www.cafepress.com/jbossorg/7125835[shirts and buttons] to give away. Our plan was almost foiled because of my absentmindedness, but it was saved when my wife delivered the "`bag of goodies`" just as the talk was ending. We spread the Arquillian swag around and it scattered off to invade other talks and strike up conversations.

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/judcon2010/dan-tossing-t-shirts.jpg" title="Dan tossing t-shirts" alt="Dan tossing t-shirts" />]

Everytime you turned around, Ike was hitching a ride on someone’s backpack.

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/judcon2010/ike-riding-on-backpack.jpg" title="Ike pin on backpack" alt="Ike pin on backpack" />]

Making his second apparence on the http://asylum.libsyn.com/[JBoss Community Asylum], this time under the JBoss moniker, Aslak explained how Arquillian makes the task of testing Java EE applications simple and possible. This would be one of many trips to the microphone throughout the week. But this time, he caught some grief from the jokester hosts poking fun at the project name:

[quote]
____

Who is Hark William? Is he a country singer or some sort of character from Star Wars?

____

Aslak set the record straight.

[quote]
____

His name is Ike. And he’s from Hollywood. He’s an alien that controls everything inside his little world.

____

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/arquillian_icon_64px.png" class="graphic" title="Arquillian icon" alt="Arquillian icon" />]

If Ike could speak, he would have simply issued the warning:

[quote]
____

Don’t mock me.

____

He must have left quite an impression, because the host immediately credited Arquillian as having "`a couple of talks today.`" Sure, that’s how we remember it too

Even though the Arquillian team only had one talk at JUDCon, we did have a front row seat in the https://jboss.org/dms/judcon/presentations/JUDCon2010_JCAConnectingEnterprise.pdf[Code generation world premiere] presented by https://community.jboss.org/people/jesper.pedersen[Jesper Pedersen] from the link:http://jboss.org/ironjacamar[IronJacamar,title="JBoss JCA"] Team, showing of their shiny new tool for JCA adaptor source code generation with full Arquillian test support.

So what’s the Arquillian project status? "`Alpha3 is coming soon. And more containers.`" (Little did they know, three containers would be added later that night).

The podcast had interrupted the JUDCon hackfest. It then proceeded to ultimately disrupt the hackfest when Emmanuel announced at the top of the hour,

[quote]
____

Let’s go get some beers.

____

So all the talk of a hackfest ended up being just that, talk. But wait, not those hard-core Arquillian developers. They’re the only ones that stuck around, pressing on even in the absense of beer.

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/judcon2010/arquillian-hackfest.jpg" title="Arquillian hackfest" alt="Arquillian hackfest" />]

"`Why?`" you ask. Because we were hell-bent on getting the Tomcat and Jetty container integrations working. I did the Jetty integration while https://community.jboss.org/people/deruelle_jean[Jean Deruelle] worked on the one for Tomcat. https://community.jboss.org/people/lincolnthree[Lincoln Baxter III] looked up from his work laying down the foundation for http://jboss.org/forge[JBoss Forge].

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/judcon2010/arquillian-hackfest-intense.jpg" title="Developers looking at screen" alt="Developers looking at screen" />]

Once we both got green bars, we finally called it a night and headed out for a round of drinks to celebrate.

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/judcon2010/drinking-at-whiskey-priest.jpg" title="Drinks at Whiskey Priest" alt="Drinks at Whiskey Priest" />]

We took a break the next afternoon to check out the sights and sounds of downtown Boston, which could be seen off in the distance from the conference center.

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/redhatsummit2010/downtown-boston.jpg" title="Boston skyscape" alt="Boston skyscape" />]

That evening I headed over to the Red Hat Summit kickoff at the pavilion for my booth duty. https://community.jboss.org/people/kenglxn[Ken Gullaksen], one of our top community members, snapped a shot of me sporting our cool https://jboss.org/coolstuff/desktopwallpapers/arquillian.html[Arquillian desktop wallpaper] on my Android phone.

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/redhatsummit2010/arquillian-android-background.jpg" title="Arquillian desktop wallpaper on Android" alt="Arquillian desktop wallpaper on Android" />]

Fashioning the Arquillian button, I was soon approached by an attendee who asked me if I had any extra. I asked him if he knew what they were for. He replied,

[quote]
____

No. But it looks really cool.

____

Ike, such a developer magnet pass:[~;)]

At some point we made our way from the conference center to a bar downtown. Like always, I’m in the bar ranting on about some technology, probably Maven:

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/redhatsummit2010/dan-ranting-at-bar.jpg" title="Dan ranting at bar" alt="Dan ranting at bar" />]

We were up early the next morning for the second Arquillian charge. ALR kicked off the conference by showing how developers can achieve rapid development with Enterprise Java by http://www.redhat.com/promo/summit/2010/presentations/jbossworld/developer-insights-ii/wed/arubginer-1020-throwing/arubinger_1020_throwing.pdf[throwing complexity over the wall] and using ShrinkWrap and Arquillian, while at the same time https://community.jboss.org/people/marklittle[Mark Little], our director, was in the other room explaining how Arquillian fits into our strategy in his http://www.redhat.com/promo/summit/2010/presentations/jbossworld/enterprise-insights/wed/mlittle-1020-state/mlittle_1020_jboss_state.pdf[JBoss State of the Union address].

[quote]
____

Arquillian is *central* to the evolution of platfoms & relevant for development.

____

To put it more bluntly, he slipped out,

[quote]
____

*You need to test the hell out of things.*

____

We agree pass:[~:)]

Handling all that complexity for the developer is hard work, though. It really took it’s toll on ALR,

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/redhatsummit2010/alr-passed-out.jpg" title="ALR passed out on couch" alt="ALR passed out on couch" />]

Arquillian got a mention in the joint http://www.redhat.com/promo/summit/2010/sessions/jboss.html#580123[RichFaces 4 / Seam Faces] talk and the http://www.redhat.com/promo/summit/2010/sessions/jboss.html#546873[Seam 3: State of Union] talk. All the activity had the developers’ interest piqued. When https://community.jboss.org/people/petemuir[Pete Muir] asked during the the http://www.redhat.com/promo/summit/2010/sessions/jboss.html#523000[JBoss Developer Story BOF] what people wanted to hear more about, someone called out,

[quote]
____

I want to hear more about the testing tool.

____

Once again, Aslak was called to the mic to showcase Arquillian. The timing was excellent because https://community.jboss.org/people/burrsutter[Burr Sutter], who is currently focused on improving the developer experience, witnessed first-hand how desperately this tool is needed. Afterwards, I said to him,

[quote]
____

It’s like we just showed up with food to a starving country.

____

And what are they asking for? *More.*

Until we meet up again, we’ll be hard at work making testing as easy and powerful as possible. That means working closely with our tools guy, https://community.jboss.org/people/maxandersen[Max Andersen] (left).

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/redhatsummit2010/max-aslak-alr.jpg" title="Max, Aslak and ALR" alt="Max, Aslak and ALR" />]

Ike sure had a big week. So at the end of it all, we bought him a drink (at Drink).

pass:[<img src="/images/blog/redhatsummit2010/arquillian-drink.jpg" title="Arquillian t-shirt with drink" alt="Arquillian t-shirt with drink" />]

The http://community.jboss.org/community/arquillian[Arquillian forums] are really heating up! Community participation is what makes the success of Arquillian possible. Keep it up folks!
