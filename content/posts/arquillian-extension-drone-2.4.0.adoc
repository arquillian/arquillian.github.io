=== Significant changes and additions:

<i class="icon-star"></i> Upgraded Selenium to 3.5.1

<i class="icon-star"></i> Improved the automatic binary download feature making it thread-safe which means that you can run your tests in parallel without any difficulty

<i class="icon-star"></i> Added support for `FirefoxOptions` and `SafariOptions` - how to use them, please take a look at the link:http://arquillian.org/arquillian-extension-drone/#_options_for_other_drivers[Drone documentation]

We hope that you'll enjoy our new stuff and look forward to hear your feedback.
