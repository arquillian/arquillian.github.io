We have bunch of small but important improvements in our extension for all Seam 2 users out there.

[discrete]
=== Some of the highlights in this release

pass:[<i class="icon-star">]pass:[</i>] Injection of pass:[<code>Identity</code>] is now supported in the Arquillian powered tests.

pass:[<i class="icon-star">]pass:[</i>] Your tests can now exercise conversation scoped components!

pass:[<i class="icon-star">]pass:[</i>] Seam 2.3 and JBoss AS 7 are now covered by our test suite thanks to https://github.com/tveronese[Thiago Veronese]

pass:[<i class="icon-star">]pass:[</i>] Seam 2 autopacking based on Maven can now use arbitraty pass:[<code>settings.xml</code>] when specified through pass:[<code>mvn.alternate.settings</code>] system property.

We look forward to hearing your feedback about this release in the http://community.jboss.org/en/arquillian[community forums]!
